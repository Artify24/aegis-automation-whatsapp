# Official Business Policies & Customer Terms

> **Entity**: Aegis Workspace Solutions (and configured multi-tenant client profiles)  
> **Effective Date**: Current Operating Year  
> **Jurisdiction**: Commercial Trade & Indian DPDP Act / GDPR Guidelines

---

## 1. Return & Replacement Policy
- **Coverage**: We offer a **7-Day Hassle-Free Replacement Guarantee** for any items that arrive with manufacturing defects, shipping/transit damage, or finish blemishes.
- **Eligibility Criteria**:
  - The request must be submitted within 7 calendar days of delivery.
  - The item must be in its original condition, un-assembled (unless installed by our certified technicians), and with original packaging and hardware tags intact.
- **Exclusions**: Normal wear and tear, intentional modification, or misuse beyond stated weight capacities (up to 150 kg for ergonomic seating).
- **Process**: Customers simply share 2 photos or a short video of the defect via WhatsApp to initiate instant replacement dispatch within 48 hours.

---

## 2. Order Cancellation & 100% Refund Policy
- **Standard Catalog Items**:
  - **Full 100% Refund**: Cancellations made within **24 hours** of order placement receive a complete 100% refund with zero cancellation fees.
  - **Post-24 Hour Cancellations**: If cancelled after 24 hours but prior to factory dispatch, an 8% administrative restocking fee applies. Once dispatched for delivery, the standard return policy applies.
- **Refund Timeline**: All approved refunds are credited back to the customer's original payment method or bank account within **3 to 5 business days**.

---

## 3. Bespoke & Custom Workstation Fabrication Policy
- **Custom Specifications**: Orders requiring non-standard desk dimensions, bespoke acoustic panel colors, or customized woodgrain laminate finishes require a **50% advance deposit**.
- **Cancellation Window**: Bespoke custom orders can be modified or cancelled within **24 hours** of order placement. Once factory cutting, edge-banding, and custom welding commence (after 24 hours), custom deposits become non-refundable as the materials are uniquely fabricated.

---

## 4. Delivery, Transit & Free Onsite Installation Policy
- **Delivery Scope**: We provide turnkey delivery and logistics across Bangalore (including Indiranagar, Whitefield, Koramangala, Electronic City, HSR Layout, and Hebbal), as well as interstate shipping across India.
- **Shipping Fees**:
  - **Orders above ₹10,000**: **FREE Delivery** across Bangalore urban limits.
  - **Orders below ₹10,000**: Flat standard nominal shipping charge of **₹999**.
- **Complimentary Onsite Installation**: All motorized standing desks, modular workstations, and boardroom conference tables include **100% Free Professional Onsite Assembly** by our factory-trained technicians.
- **Lead Time**: In-stock catalog items are delivered within **3 to 5 business days**. Custom corporate projects are delivered within **10 to 14 business days**.

---

## 5. Privacy, Data Retention & Customer Security Policy
- **DPDP & GDPR Compliance**: We strictly adhere to the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and global privacy standards.
- **Zero Third-Party Sharing**: We do not sell, rent, or trade customer contact details, phone numbers, or corporate purchase orders to any third-party marketing brokers.
- **PII Phone Masking**: All customer telephone numbers and sensitive identifiers are masked in operational system logs (e.g., `9198****3210`).
- **Right to Erasure**: Customers have the right to request complete deletion of their conversation history and CRM record at any time by replying *ERASE DATA* or contacting privacy compliance.

  - **50+ Enterprise Workstations**: Custom project pricing with dedicated account management and 3D architectural layout planning.
